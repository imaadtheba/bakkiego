Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

BakkieGo ("we", "our", "the app") connects traders and drivers for on-demand delivery services in South Africa. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Phone number, name, and role (trader or driver)
  • For drivers: vehicle model, colour, and license plate
  • Job details you post or accept: pickup and delivery addresses, load size, agreed price
  • Location data associated with delivery addresses, used only for job matching and navigation
  • Device push notification tokens, used to notify you of job updates

How We Use This Information

  • To connect traders with available drivers
  • To display driver and vehicle details to traders for identification and safety
  • To send notifications about job status (accepted, on the way, completed, cancelled)
  • To calculate suggested prices based on delivery area

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties
  • We do not process payments — all payments are made directly between trader and driver in cash

Data Storage

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, our database provider. Job and account data is retained to provide the service and may be deleted on request.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at hello@bakkiego.co.za.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@bakkiego.co.za.
